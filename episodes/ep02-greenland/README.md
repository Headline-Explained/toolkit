# Why Trump Wanted Greenland (And What He Actually Got)

**Series:** World News · **Video:** https://youtu.be/A21QnKsL6wQ (public from 1 Dec 2026) · facts as of the date in the video description

On most flat maps, Greenland looks as big as Africa. It isn't — Africa is about 14 times bigger. But on 19 November 2026, Denmark's parliament voted 109–0 to let the United States expand its military presence on the island. Here's what Greenland is, why America has wanted it since 1867, what the new US–Denmark–Greenland deal actually says, who won, and what to watch next.

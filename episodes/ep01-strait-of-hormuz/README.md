# 21 Miles That Control the World's Oil: The Strait of Hormuz, Explained

**Series:** World News · **Video:** https://youtu.be/ShA_8ioajog (public from 24 Nov 2026) · facts as of the date in the video description

Before the war, about 20 million barrels of oil a day, roughly a fifth of everything the world uses, moved through a strip of sea about 21 miles wide. In 2026 that strait became a battlefield. Here is what the Strait of Hormuz is, why there is no easy way around it, how the US–Iran war got us here, why oil prices are falling even as attacks rise, and the three ways this could go next.

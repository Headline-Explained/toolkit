## ✅ 5 signs to watch
Tick them off when you see them in the news. They tell you which of the three roads we're on.

- [ ] **Power deals:** tech companies buying the output of whole power plants, even nuclear ones
- [ ] **Chip rules:** new limits from Washington, or answers from Beijing
- [ ] **Entry-level hiring:** payroll and job-posting data for office jobs most exposed to AI
- [ ] **The spending:** if big tech cuts its AI budgets, road two (the wall) is coming
- [ ] **The gap:** Chinese models matching American ones on weaker chips points to road three (a split)

**The three roads:** (1) it keeps speeding up · (2) it hits a wall: power, chips or money · (3) the world splits into
an American and a Chinese AI system.

## 📖 Words from this episode
- **GPU:** a graphics chip; it turned out to be perfect for AI maths
- **Transformer:** the 2017 design behind today's chatbots (the T in GPT)
- **Data centre:** a building full of computers; the biggest AI ones draw close to a gigawatt
- **Export controls:** rules that stop advanced chips being sold to certain countries
- **AI agent:** an AI that carries out tasks step by step, not just answers questions

## 🕰️ Timeline
| Year | What happened |
|---|---|
| 1950 | Alan Turing asks "Can machines think?" |
| 1956 | Dartmouth workshop names "artificial intelligence" |
| 1970s, late 1980s | Two "AI winters": funding dries up |
| 1997 | IBM's Deep Blue beats chess champion Garry Kasparov |
| 2012 | AlexNet, trained on two gaming GPUs, wins the ImageNet contest |
| 2016 | DeepMind's AlphaGo beats Lee Sedol at Go |
| 2017 | Google's "Attention Is All You Need" introduces the Transformer |
| 2022 | US restricts advanced AI chips to China (Oct); ChatGPT launches (30 Nov) |
| 2025 | DeepSeek's model rivals US leaders; Nvidia loses ~$589 bn in a day (27 Jan) |
| 2026 | ~900 m weekly ChatGPT users; ~$700 bn of big-tech spending; gigawatt AI campuses |

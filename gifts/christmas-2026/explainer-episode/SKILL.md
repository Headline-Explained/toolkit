# /explainer-episode <topic>
The Headline Explained method, shared as a Christmas gift (2026). It is the working method, not our video engine:
use whatever editor or code renderer you like, and check its licence and any voice or picture licence yourself.

Work in one folder per episode. A stage starts only when the one before passes. After three failed fix rounds, stop and say why.

## Ask the maker only here
1. The finished video.
2. The thumbnail (offer 2 or 3).
3. Before anything is uploaded, scheduled or published.
Also stop for: a fact with no source, an unclear licence, any cost.

## Stages
| # | Do | Pass when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `research.md`: a table of claim, source, date, grade. Rank the 10 most surprising true facts | every claim has a source |
| 2 | `story.md`: one sentence "X AND Y, BUT Z, THEREFORE W", one question, one object you follow from first shot to last | the question fits in one line |
| 3 | `script.md` | script checks below |
| 4 | `factcheck.md`: each line of the script against research | nothing unverified is in the script |
| 5 | Pictures with `credits.json`: file, author, licence, link | no unknown or non-commercial licence if you earn money |
| 6 | Voice, then time every word | one voice, never mixed |
| 7 | `shots.json` | shot checks below, three review rounds |
| 8 | Make the picture; look at still frames of every part | nothing overlaps, floats or freezes |
| 9 | Description: hook, chapters, sources, credits, honest AI note | links work |
| 10 | Thumbnails: 4 words at most | readable at 320 px wide |
| 11 | Full render; check length, loudness (about -14 LUFS), dark or still frames | all pass |
| 12 | Show the maker. Upload only after a clear yes | |

Facts: use two sources for any surprising claim or any number in a title or thumbnail. Say how a figure is measured.
Label opinion ("My read:"). Write "Facts as of <date>".

## Script checks
- Open inside a moment (a time, a place, something happening), not a topic. Hook in 20 seconds; promise by 40.
- Every part has: WANT / OBSTACLE / TURN.
- Join beats with "but" or "so", not "and then".
- One idea per sentence; aim for 18 words or fewer.
- A second surprise around the second minute; a twist at about 60 to 80 percent.
- Open a question, close it, keep one or two open. Close all of them by the end.
- Say "you" about once a minute. Give every big number something to picture.
- End on the opening image, changed, then ask one real question.

## Shot checks
- Each shot says: the words it covers, who does what, what moves, the camera move, and why it follows the last shot.
- Split or add a second action when a shot runs past about 5 seconds.
- Story scenes fill the frame and have depth: far, middle, subject, front.
- Never the same camera move twice in a row. A still camera needs a reason.
- **No device twice too often:** one kind of insert (a letter, a card, a chart) at most twice per video.
  Find a new action for each: a message can be typed, stamped, torn, posted or sketched.
- **Props act:** each prop needs a hand holding it, an action, and an effect (steam, splash, sparks, dust, a sound).
- Photos and flat cards: 15 percent of a part at most. Give people hands, faces and reactions.
- Label anything imagined as an illustration. Credit every real picture.

## Words on screen
- Show each spoken word once, never twice (no burned captions plus a subtitle track drawn over them).
- One or two power words per line may act (fall, stamp, glow), with a soft sound.
- Keep text clear of faces and the main action; check it on a still.

## Shorts
- 30 to 60 seconds, one idea, hook in the first 2 seconds, reframed for 9:16, ending with a pointer to the full video.

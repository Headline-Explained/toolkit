# Prompts for reading the news with AI

Copy, paste, fill in the [brackets]. Works in any AI chatbot. Always check important facts against the original source.

1. **Summary + what's missing:** "Here is a news article: [paste]. Summarise it in 5 bullet points. Then tell me what important context, or which other side of the story, is missing."
2. **Both sides:** "Give me the strongest argument for and the strongest argument against [policy or claim], each in 3 sentences, without taking a side."
3. **Check a number:** "This article says [number + claim]. What would I need to check to know whether it's accurate, and where would I find the original data?"
4. **Map it:** "Which countries are involved in [story], what does each one want, and what can each one actually do about it?"
5. **What to watch:** "Based on [story], what are 3 concrete signs I could watch for in the next 6 months that would tell me where it's heading?"
